PathFolk Support

Need help with your account, an appointment request, a membership, or a report? Email us at support@pathfolk.app. We reply within 2 business days. Please do not include Social Security, bank or card numbers.

Quick answers

  • Delete your account: open PathFolk, go to Account, and tap Delete my account. You will be asked for your password. Everything tied to your account is deleted.
  • Report or block someone: open the conversation in Messages and tap Report or Block.
  • Cancel an agent membership: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose PathFolk, and tap Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password. We email you a reset link.

PathFolk is a directory and booking tool. It does not sell insurance, give quotes or advice, or take applications or premiums.
